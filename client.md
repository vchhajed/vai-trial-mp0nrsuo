- **pricing transparency:** At Trial, we believe in transparency regarding all costs and fees associated with your real estate transaction. Beyond our standard brokerage commission, clients should be aware of potential expenses including title insurance, appraisal fees, home inspection costs, property surveys, pest inspections, and closing costs typically ranging from two to five percent of the purchase price. Additionally, if financing is involved, lenders may charge origination fees, underwriting fees, and prepaid items such as property taxes and homeowners insurance. For sellers, marketing expenses and any necessary repairs or concessions to buyers may apply. We recommend discussing your specific situation with one of our agents, as costs can vary based on local regulations, property type, and individual transaction details. Our team is committed to helping you understand every expense upfront so there are no surprises at closing.
